# 6. Design Reflection

## 1. What are your two selected features?

My two selected features are adding a tree, and viewing my logged trees on the platform.

## 2. Why did you select these over the other eight requirements?

I selected these two features as they form the base functionality of what my app will do.
Utilizing the `MoSCoW` method was very helpful in prioritizing my two selected features. The two features I have selected will also be the easiest to implement, by building the API structure from the ground up. 


## 3. How do they fit into your overall app architecture?

As the two features I have selected are the basic add and read functionality of the app, some of my other 8 unselected features will depend on them. For instance features such as filtering and searching trees based on specific criteria and logging maintenance reports will heavily depend on trees being added to the app. 


## 4. What do you expect to be the most challenging part of implementing them?

<!-- Be specific and technical. This is a prediction — you will revisit it in

The most challenging part of implementing the two features may be integrating an external Map API such as MapTiler or Google Maps into my Map sections on the 'adding tree' and 'view all my trees' pages. Another complexity may be seperating the abstraction of individual pages of trees I recorded and trees that other people have recorded.
