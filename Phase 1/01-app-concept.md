# 1. App Concept

The Rākau app will be built to solve the issue of keeping a maintained record of the broad catalogue of New Zealand's exotic and native trees. The target audience will primarily be for anyone in New Zealand who wants to record individual trees. Users of this application will be people such as home gardeners, arborists, local/regional council members, conservation groups or just general citizen scientists/tree enthuasists. Additional users include people who work in plant nurseries, real estate developers and even school educators. The core functionality of this application will be for users to add and record trees they have identified, such as species, location and condition. Another key functionality is allowing users to browse the catalogue of recorded trees, so they can discover more about the trees within their local area or even across New Zealand. Another key functionality is for arborists and other professional users to maintain detailed records capturing their work on particular trees. Detailed records can consist of maintenance history of trees, or even just general reports of tree health status. One more key feature of this app would be to filter tree's by species, genus, height, girth, region and even significance status.

An additional feature maybe to have a graph for each recording tree showing their health status overtime, by adding follow-up observations to an existing record. Lastly another additional feature could be a version control backlog where users can see view a history profile for each recorded tree.
